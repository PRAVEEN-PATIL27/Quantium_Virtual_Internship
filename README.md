# Quantium Virtual Internship – Retail Strategy & Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Quantium Data Analytics Virtual Experience Program**.

The project focuses on analysing customer purchasing behaviour, identifying growth opportunities, evaluating trial store performance, and providing data-driven recommendations to support Quantium's retail strategy.

The analysis was completed across multiple tasks, covering customer analytics, product and category insights, and trial store performance evaluation.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to:

- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Understand product and category preferences.
- Evaluate the performance of selected trial stores.
- Compare trial stores against suitable control stores.
- Measure the impact of the trial period on sales and customer numbers.
- Provide recommendations for future business strategy and store rollout.

---

## 📈 Task 2 – Trial Store Performance Analysis

The second stage evaluated the performance of trial stores against carefully selected control stores.

### Trial Stores Analysed

- Store 77
- Store 86
- Store 88

### Analysis Included

- Selection of comparable control stores
- Pre-trial performance comparison
- Trial period sales analysis
- Customer number analysis
- Control-adjusted performance evaluation
- Identification of statistically significant changes

### Key Findings

#### Store 77

Store 77 showed a positive customer response during the trial period when compared with its control store.

#### Store 86

Store 86 demonstrated a strong positive trial outcome, including significant improvements in sales and customer performance.

#### Store 88

Store 88 showed a strong customer baseline, but its results required further investigation before recommending a broader rollout.

---

## 💡 Final Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

### 1. Focus on High-Value Customer Segments

Prioritise customer segments that demonstrate high purchasing frequency and strong spending behaviour.

### 2. Support Successful Trial Stores

Stores 77 and 86 demonstrated positive trial performance and can be considered for a targeted expansion strategy.

### 3. Investigate Store 88

Store 88 requires further analysis before wider implementation due to differences in its baseline performance.

### 4. Use Data-Driven Rollout Strategies

Future store initiatives should continue using control store comparisons and customer-level analysis to measure business impact.
